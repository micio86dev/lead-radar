# Commercial licensing

Lead Radar is licensed under the **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0** (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)).
That license permits **noncommercial** use. Any commercial use needs a separate, paid license from the
copyright holder.

## What counts as commercial use

The license defines a commercial purpose as one "directed toward commercial advantage or monetary
compensation". In practice, a paid license is required when the software (or a derivative of it) is used
to:

- run or support a business, an agency service, or a paid consulting engagement;
- power a product, an internal revenue-generating workflow, or a managed/hosted service offered to others;
- be redistributed, resold, or bundled with a commercial offering;
- be evaluated beyond a short private trial with intent to use it commercially.

Noncommercial use stays free: personal study, research, experiments, hobby projects, and use by
charitable, educational, public-research, public-safety, health, environmental, or government
institutions, regardless of funding source.

## How to get a commercial license

Open an issue in this repository titled `Commercial license request`, or contact the maintainer through
their GitHub profile (`github.com/micio86dev`). Include: who will use the software, the product or service
it supports, the scale (tenants, leads per month, seats), and whether you need redistribution rights.
Terms and price are agreed in writing per case; nothing here is an offer or a price list.

## Why not a royalty scheme

A license cannot collect money by itself: it grants rights, and the payment is a contract between the
parties. A royalty model ("pay X% of revenue") needs a signed agreement that defines revenue, audits and
reporting, which is why commercial use is licensed per case instead.
